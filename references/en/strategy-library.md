# Strategic Scenario Library

A comprehensive mapping of 94 curated thinking models into 9 strategic scenarios. Used for automatic scenario detection and model recommendation during GROW coaching sessions.

## Overview

Each scenario contains:
- Scenario code and name
- Core description
- Signal words for auto-detection
- 10-12 recommended models with coaching steps
- Typical use cases
- Difficulty level

## 9 Strategic Scenarios

### S01: Cognitive Calibration (12 models)
- **Description**: Identify and correct thinking biases, improve judgment accuracy
- **Signal words**: "Am I wrong?", "bias", "judgment error", "why do I always"
- **Core models**: Confirmation Bias, Anchoring Effect, Availability Heuristic, Hindsight Bias, Dunning-Kruger Effect, Sunk Cost Fallacy, Framing Effect, Survivor Bias, Base Rate Fallacy, Bandwagon Effect, Authority Bias, Status Quo Bias
- **Typical questions**: "Am I making the right judgment?", "Why do I keep making the same mistake?", "Is my thinking biased?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

### S02: Learning & Growth (10 models)
- **Description**: Accelerate skill acquisition and knowledge retention
- **Signal words**: "how to learn", "improve", "master", "skill", "growth"
- **Core models**: Feynman Technique, Deliberate Practice, Pareto Principle (80/20), Spaced Repetition, Growth Mindset, Zone of Proximal Development, Mental Models Transfer, Active Recall, Interleaving Practice, Meta-Learning
- **Typical questions**: "How can I learn faster?", "How to master a new skill?", "What's the best way to study?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

### S03: Problem Solving (10 models)
- **Description**: Break through complex problems with structured thinking
- **Signal words**: "how to solve", "breakthrough", "innovate", "stuck"
- **Core models**: Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys), First Principles Thinking, Inversion Thinking, Lateral Thinking, SCAMPER, Design Thinking, Hypothesis-Driven Approach, MECE Principle, Occam's Razor, Red Teaming
- **Typical questions**: "How do I solve this problem?", "I'm stuck, what should I do?", "How to think outside the box?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

### S04: Systems Insight (10 models)
- **Description**: Understand complex systems, identify feedback loops and leverage points
- **Signal words**: "recurring", "system", "chain reaction", "why does this keep happening"
- **Core models**: Flywheel Effect, Systems Thinking, Feedback Loops, Tragedy of the Commons, Network Effects, Second-Order Thinking, Complexity Theory, Emergence, Leverage Points, Bottleneck Analysis
- **Typical questions**: "Why does this keep recurring?", "How does this system work?", "Where's the leverage point?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

### S05: Data & Evidence (10 models)
- **Description**: Evaluate data reliability, avoid statistical traps
- **Signal words**: "data", "evidence", "reliable", "statistics", "proof"
- **Core models**: Correlation ≠ Causation, Base Rate Fallacy, Simpson's Paradox, Sample Size Bias, Regression to the Mean, Cherry Picking, Texas Sharpshooter, Bayes' Theorem, Data Visualization Literacy, Statistical Significance
- **Typical questions**: "Is this data reliable?", "Can I trust this conclusion?", "Are these statistics misleading?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

### S06: Decision Analysis (12 models)
- **Description**: Make better decisions under uncertainty with structured frameworks
- **Signal words**: "choose", "decide", "weigh", "trade-off", "option"
- **Core models**: Decision Matrix, Opportunity Cost, Expected Value, Minimax Principle, Sunk Cost Fallacy, Reversibility Test, 10/10/10 Rule, Prospect Theory, Asymmetric Upside, Pre-mortem Analysis, Marginal Thinking, Opportunity Tree
- **Typical questions**: "Should I choose A or B?", "How to make this decision?", "What are the trade-offs?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

### S07: Conflict Resolution (10 models)
- **Description**: Navigate conflicts, negotiations, and difficult conversations
- **Signal words**: "conflict", "negotiation", "complaint", "disagree", "tension"
- **Core models**: Game Theory, Nash Equilibrium, BATNA, Framing Effect in Negotiation, Tit for Tat, Principled Negotiation, Empathy Mapping, Active Listening, Conflict Styles (Thomas-Kilmann), Win-Win Strategy
- **Typical questions**: "How to handle this conflict?", "How to negotiate better?", "Customer is complaining, what to do?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

### S08: Potential Release (10 models)
- **Description**: Unlock team and individual potential through effective communication and management
- **Signal words**: "team", "manage", "motivate", "lead", "communicate"
- **Core models**: Pygmalion Effect, Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, Social Proof, Reciprocity, Scarcity Principle, Authority Principle, Unity Principle, Two-Factor Theory, Situational Leadership, Psychological Safety
- **Typical questions**: "How to motivate my team?", "How to communicate better?", "How to be a better leader?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

### S09: Market Strategy (10 models)
- **Description**: Build competitive advantages and market positioning
- **Signal words**: "competitor", "advantage", "differentiation", "market", "strategy"
- **Core models**: Economic Moat, Network Effects, Switching Costs, Economies of Scale, Brand Power, Blue Ocean Strategy, Value Chain Analysis, Porter's Five Forces, First-Mover Advantage, Platform Strategy
- **Typical questions**: "How to beat competitors?", "What's our competitive advantage?", "How to differentiate?"
- **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

## Scenario Matching Rules

During Phase 0 (Problem Identification), match scenarios based on:

| Problem Feature | Match Scenario | Signal Words |
|---------------|---------------|-------------|
| Personal judgment/bias | S01 Cognitive Calibration | "Am I wrong", "bias", "judgment error" |
| Learning/improvement | S02 Learning & Growth | "how to learn", "improve", "master" |
| Problem solving | S03 Problem Solving | "how to solve", "breakthrough", "stuck" |
| System/cycles | S04 Systems Insight | "recurring", "system", "chain reaction" |
| Data/evidence | S05 Data & Evidence | "data", "evidence", "reliable", "statistics" |
| Choices/trade-offs | S06 Decision Analysis | "choose", "decide", "weigh", "trade-off" |
| Conflict/negotiation | S07 Conflict Resolution | "conflict", "negotiation", "complaint" |
| Team/management | S08 Potential Release | "team", "manage", "motivate", "lead" |
| Competition/market | S09 Market Strategy | "competitor", "advantage", "differentiation" |

**Multiple scenarios can be matched simultaneously** for complex problems (primary + secondary scenarios).

```
                    S01 Cognitive

          S06 Decision        S07 Conflict
           |        \         /        |
    S05 Data ←-- S03 Problem --→ S08 Team
           \        /         \        /
          S04 Systems        S09 Market
                   \            /
```

**Recommended learning paths:**
- Beginner: S01 → S06 → S08 (most common workplace scenarios)
- Intermediate: S03 → S05 → S07 (analysis and conflict skills)
- Advanced: S04 → S09 (systems thinking and strategy)
- Full mastery: All 9 scenarios, focus on cross-scenario model combinations
